Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

CleanCrew Empire collects information needed to process purchases and provide support. Stripe processes payment-card data; CleanCrew Empire does not need to store full card numbers in the website code.

Information used

Purchase email, Stripe Checkout Session identifiers, package purchased, payment amount/currency, delivery status, and support communications may be recorded for fulfillment, fraud prevention, customer service, and accounting.

Service providers

Information may be processed by service providers used for hosting, payment processing, Google Sheets/Apps Script, email delivery, and file storage.

Retention and security

Records may be retained as reasonably necessary for transactions, support, legal obligations, and fraud prevention. Sensitive secret keys are not intended to be stored in public browser-side code.

Contact

Contact support to ask privacy-related questions or request assistance with information associated with your purchase, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.
